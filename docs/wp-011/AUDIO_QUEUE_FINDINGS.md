# WP-011 Technical Findings — Unified Loading, Paced Sequential TTS & Rate-Limit Recovery

## 1. Root Cause & Unified Loading Fix
- **Why turns failed intermittently:** Earlier WP-011 playback mounted the studio immediately and fired TTS requests at `MAX_TTS_CONCURRENCY = 2` with zero spacing, tripping Google free-tier Gemini TTS RPM/RPD limits and prematurely shutting down hosted playback after `2` capacity failures. Maya appeared often simply because the explainer role carries later turns after burst exhaustion.
- **Unified continuous loading panel (`src/lib/episode/unified-loading-state.ts`, `src/components/kuro/generation-status.tsx`):** Removed the legacy separate preparation panel (`audio-preparation-panel.tsx`). Script generation (`Writing script and Kuromate turns`), schema validation (`Validating episode structure`), sequential opening-voice preparation (`Preparing opening Kuromate voices`), and ready/partial/unavailable actions now stay inside **one `"Preparing your conversation"` card**. A compact idle Kuromate preview shows cast count and roles without `ON AIR`, `Speaking`, turn counters, visuals, or transport buttons.
- **Studio mount gating (`src/components/kuro/kuro-skeleton.tsx`, `src/components/kuro/player/episode-player.tsx`):** Full podcast studio mounts strictly when `episode !== null && queueSnapshot?.hasStartedPodcast === true`. Silent caption fallback renders **`TIMED CAPTIONS`** and Kuromate **`Captioning`** (static badge, no `Speaking` or `● ON AIR`), and [CaptionPanel](../../src/components/kuro/player/caption-panel.tsx) clears audio-unavailable notices once audio recovers.

## 2. Sequential Concurrency, 1,500 ms Pacing & Opening Buffer
- **Locked configuration ([tts-pacing-config.ts](../../src/lib/tts/tts-pacing-config.ts)):**
  - `MAX_TTS_CONCURRENCY = 1` (single active `POST /api/tts` request at a time).
  - `MIN_TTS_REQUEST_INTERVAL_MS = 1_500` (`KURO_TTS_REQUEST_INTERVAL_MS` clamped server-side to `[1000, 5000]` ms, passed numerically from [page.tsx](../../src/app/page.tsx), never via `NEXT_PUBLIC_*`).
  - `INITIAL_AUDIO_BUFFER_SIZE = 2` (`1` for a single-turn episode), `PREFETCH_LOOKAHEAD = 2`, `MAX_AUTOMATIC_CAPACITY_RETRIES_PER_SEGMENT = 1`, `Retry-After` cooldown clamped `[1000, 30000]` ms (fallback `8000` ms).
- **Stage progression & recovery buttons:** Opening turns `1` and `2` decode-check via `verifyPlayable` sequentially (`0 of 2 ready` -> `1 of 2 ready` -> `2 of 2 ready` -> `"Ready to begin"` / **`Start podcast`**). If Turn `1` succeeds and Turn `2` hits `429`, `"Rate limit wait"` displays `"One opening voice is ready. Waiting briefly for the next turn."`, retries once after cooldown, and on partial exhaustion offers **`Start with available audio and captions`**, **`Continue preparing voices`**, and **`Cancel and edit`**. Permanent missing-key (`503`) or auth (`401`/`403`) failures stop after `1` call and show **`Continue with timed captions`** and **`Return to form`** with zero retry buttons.
- **Single scheduler:** Pre-start prep, lookahead `[currentIndex .. currentIndex + 2]`, **Replay**, and **`Retry voice`** share one `pacingTimer` + `cooldownTimer`. Input validation rejections and cache hits skip pacing.

## 3. Non-Fatal 429s, Failure Classification & Provider-Call Bounds
- **Transient vs. permanent failures (`SegmentFailureCategory = "transient" | "permanent"`):** HTTP `429` (`TTS_RATE_LIMITED`) uses shared `Retry-After`, retries once automatically, and falls back to timed captions for *that turn only* without killing later turns. Permanent shutdown stops immediately on `TTS_NOT_CONFIGURED` (`503`), auth `401`/`403`, non-retryable `TTS_PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`, or `>= 2` consecutive non-429 errors.
- **Provider-call bounds:** Each segment takes up to `2` automatic client calls (`1` initial + `1` post-cooldown retry), `3` typical / `4` max upstream provider calls. Episode automatic ceilings: Quick `8`-turn (`8` ideal -> `16` max client -> `24` typical / `32` max upstream); Detailed `15`-turn (`15` ideal -> `30` max client -> `45` typical / `60` max upstream).
- **Manual retry, Replay & cleanup:** **`Retry voice`** (`1` attempt/cycle) respects cooldown and `1,500 ms` pacing and preserves cache; **Replay** reuses cached WAVs (`0` refetches) and sequentially retries eligible transient misses. `createEpisodeObjectUrlPool` revokes every blob URL once; cancel, back, completion, and unmount abort fetches and clear timers cleanly.
